Design notes

The owner side of StayEasy Helper: a messaging-style assistant for property owners. It answers the questions owners ask most (what will I be paid, who's staying, how am I doing, is that repair fixed?) and brings in their account manager for anything about money disputes or damage.

Evidence behind the design

    Design decisions

    • Every pound is explained. Payouts show bookings, commission, cleaning and any repairs line by line, so owners don't need to ask what a deduction was for.
    • Show the impact before the action. Blocking dates or changing prices shows the likely effect on earnings before the owner confirms.
    • The assistant suggests, the owner decides. Pricing tips explain the data behind them, nothing changes without a tap, and every change can be undone.
    • Not every tip is a discount. If a home is in demand, the assistant suggests raising the price.
    • Issues are tracked, not just logged. Each report gets a number and live steps, and is prioritised by when the next guests arrive.
    • People for money disputes and damage. Questions about deductions and damage claims go straight to the account manager, with the statement and booking already shared.
    • Urgent skips the menus. Words like “leak” or “no heating” jump to the on-call team.
    • One data set, two sides. Priya's Rose Cottage stay (SE-10482) is the same booking she manages in the guest prototype.
